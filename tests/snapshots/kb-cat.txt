## Charter

The program exists to find a thermostable lead from the NW-200 scaffold series for oral delivery (helios-program-charter.pdf › block 1). It is headed by [[Dana Whitfield]] (helios-program-charter.pdf › block 2).

<!-- sec:sec_budget -->
## Budget

Fiscal 2024 funding is USD 2.4 million, reviewed each quarter by the portfolio committee (helios-program-charter.pdf › block 3).

<!-- sec:sec_success_criterion -->
## Success criterion

A lead must keep at least 80 percent of its activity after two weeks at 40 degrees Celsius (helios-program-charter.pdf › block 4).
